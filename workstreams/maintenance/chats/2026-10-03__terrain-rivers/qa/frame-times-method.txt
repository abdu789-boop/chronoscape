Frame timing method (scratch harness, not committed): headless Chrome at 1440 x 900 CSS
pixels and device pixel ratio 2, GPU rendering. The published site (git archive of f752a39)
and the working tree were served from disk through Playwright request routing, with .webp
served as image/webp. This change was measured with its default layers, terrain and rivers
on, after waiting for the river key to appear and then 2.5 s for the relief.

Each requestAnimationFrame callback's CPU time was recorded for, in order:
  drag         40 pointer moves across the map
  still frame  500 ms after release: the full-detail frame that follows movement
  key pan      an arrow key while the map still has focus from the drag, which pans the
               map: a fast frame and, within 400 ms, its full-detail frame
  year step    the same key after removing focus from the map, which steps to the next map
               year; the harness checks that the year face changed
  wheel        12 wheel steps zooming in, 20 ms apart
Four views: 1200 CE at world scale; 1700 CE at zoom 3.5 over central Europe; the globe in
1200 CE; the globe at zoom 3. Two runs per view and version.

Values are local runs and vary between runs. They measure canvas command time on this
machine, not GPU completion, load time or a frame-rate guarantee.

Correction to the 2026-10-01 Folio method: it pressed the arrow key immediately after the
drag, while the map had focus, so its "year step" was a keyboard pan.
