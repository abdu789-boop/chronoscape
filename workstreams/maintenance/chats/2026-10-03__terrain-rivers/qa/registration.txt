Registration check (scratch harness, not committed), 2026-10-03.
Headless Chrome, 1440 x 900, device pixel ratio 1, local server, light theme,
labels and cities off. Each case loads a view, moves it with the pointer, moves
the pointer off the map and removes keyboard focus (so no hover outline or focus
ring), waits 1.2 s for the full-detail frame and captures the map area. It then
opens the resulting link in a new page and captures the same area. Cached river
paths, the relief and the other layers must land in the same place.

Cases and resulting links:
  flat pan    #year=1700&zoom=3.5&lon=15&lat=48, dragged 180 x 80 px
              -> #year=1700&zoom=3.5&lon=-6.71&lat=55.748&cities=0&labels=0
  flat wheel  #year=1700&zoom=2&lon=15&lat=48, six wheel steps in
              -> #year=1700&zoom=3.432&lon=11.041&lat=47.13&cities=0&labels=0
  globe drag  #year=1700&view=globe&zoom=2&rx=-20&ry=-40, dragged 160 x 60 px
              -> #year=1700&view=globe&zoom=2&lon=-16.656&lat=5.83&rx=-3.452&ry=-46.205&cities=0&labels=0
