Frame timing method (scratch harness, not committed): headless Chrome at 1440 x 900 CSS
pixels and device pixel ratio 2. The current site (git archive of HEAD before this change)
and the working tree were served from disk through Playwright request routing. Each
requestAnimationFrame callback's CPU time was recorded while the mouse dragged the map for
40 moves, then for 500 ms after release (the full-detail frame that follows movement), and
for one previous/next year step. Two views: 1200 CE at world scale, and 1700 CE at zoom 3.5
over central Europe. Values are single local runs and vary between runs (an earlier run gave
5.5 ms and 12.1 ms for the settle frame and 15.6 ms and 25.1 ms for the year step). They
measure canvas command time on this machine, not GPU completion, end-to-end load time or a
frame-rate guarantee. "settle: null" for the current site means it has no separate frame.
