      Controls and data

      The map displays historical territories, cities, and population estimates from 3400 BCE to 2024 CE.

      Navigation. Drag to pan and scroll or pinch to zoom. Choose Map or Globe to change the projection.

      Search. Search finds polities by name or alternative name, places, sourced rulers, and years such as “500 BCE.”

      Selection. Select a territory or a search result to view its recorded dates, mapped area, rulers, and inferred territorial relationships. Play steps through its mapped years.

      What changed. This list compares the current map with the one before it: territories first mapped, no longer mapped, or changed in area.

      Timeline. Drag the scale, enter a year, or press play. Hatching above the scale shows how often the map changes. Narrow the timeline window for finer control.
