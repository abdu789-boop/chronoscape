    Controls and data

    The map displays historical territories, cities, and population estimates from 3400 BCE to 2024 CE.

    Navigation. Drag to pan and scroll or pinch to zoom. Choose Map or Globe to change the projection.

    Selection. Select a territory or search by name to view its recorded dates, mapped area, and inferred territorial relationships.

    Timeline. Drag the slider, enter a year such as “500 BCE,” or press play. Narrow the timeline window for finer control.
